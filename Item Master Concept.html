What changed, and why

  • Four sections instead of three columnsIdentity, units, pricing and suppliers each get a full-width home with a rail that shows what is done. Nothing is squeezed into a third of a dialog.
  • Units live with stockThe base unit is a chip ladder in plain words, defaults are two selects instead of four checkboxes, and reorder levels sit beside the unit they are counted in.
  • Three entry points, not four modesAdding a supplier price hides everything else. A name match offers a switch rather than silently replacing the form.
  • Errors where the field isInline messages, a counted list in the summary, and a dot on the section. No banner pushing the form down.
  • Money in contextThe summary shows the tax-inclusive price, best supplier cost and margin as you type. Supplier prices say which unit they are for.
  • Locking explainedWhen stock or documents use the item, base unit and factors lock with the reason and the way out, instead of a failure on save.
Things I noticed in the current code
  • submit(keepOpen) never reads _keepOpen, so Save & Add Another closes the dialog. Here it clears the form and keeps it open.
  • description, minStock, reorderPoint and reorderQty are in form state and sent to the API, but no input renders them.
  • Edit saves the item, then fires one request per supplier row. A single updateWithSupplierItems mutation in one transaction would avoid half-saved items.
  • ExistingPreview repeats data the form already shows. The summary panel replaces it.
